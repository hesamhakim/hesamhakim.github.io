Whoever runs it is responsible for the content they upload and for complying with YouTube's Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. The application sets YouTube's synthetic-media disclosure on every upload, because its narration is generated rather than recorded.

The videos summarise other people's published research. Each names the paper, its DOI and its licence. They are not endorsed by, nor produced with, the papers' authors. Where a paper is published under a Creative Commons licence, its figures and data are used under that licence and credited accordingly.
